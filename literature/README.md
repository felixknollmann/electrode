# Literature

The papers below are the basis for the physics in this package. They are not redistributed
here for copyright reasons; follow the links.

- **R. Schmied**, *Electrostatics of gapped and finite surface electrodes*,
  New J. Phys. **12**, 023038 (2010).
  DOI: [10.1088/1367-2630/12/2/023038](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/12/2/023038)
  · arXiv: [0910.4517](https://arxiv.org/abs/0910.4517).
  Used for: the gap interpolation potential (Eq. 6), the gap polarization potential
  (Eqs. 9, 21), nulling the surface charge at gap centres (§II D), the gap susceptibility
  (§II E, Fig. 7), and the closed-form test cases (Eqs. 8, 14, 15, 18).
- **J. H. Wesenberg**, *Electrostatics of surface-electrode ion traps*,
  Phys. Rev. A **78**, 063410 (2008).
  DOI: [10.1103/PhysRevA.78.063410](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.78.063410).
  Used for: the gapless plane approximation, and the solid-angle / Biot–Savart form of the
  potential (Eqs. 3–5).
- **M. H. Oliveira and J. A. Miranda**, *Biot–Savart-like law in electrostatics*,
  Eur. J. Phys. **22**, 31 (2001). Wesenberg's Ref. [16]; the source of the
  edge-sum form of the potential.
